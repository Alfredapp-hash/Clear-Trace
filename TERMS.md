# ClearTrace Terms of Use (Operator Draft)

**Version:** 1.8.1 · **Last updated:** 2026-10-12

This document is a **draft** for self-hosted deployments, including a copy you run yourself to evaluate the software. It is not legal advice. Consult qualified counsel before commercial distribution.

## 1. Authorized use

ClearTrace is designed for **authorized privacy remediation** — acting on your own information or on behalf of someone you are legally permitted to represent. You must not use ClearTrace to investigate, harass, or target unrelated individuals.

## 2. No legal advice

ClearTrace provides workflow tooling, draft templates, and audit logs. It does **not** provide legal advice. Review all correspondence before sending. Escalation templates require human and legal review.

## 3. Your connectors and data

You supply API keys and OAuth credentials (BYOK). You are responsible for provider terms, rate limits, and lawful use of third-party services. Identity claims are encrypted at rest in your deployment; you control backup and retention.

## 4. Outbound communication

ClearTrace does not send email or webhook events unless you opt in. Gmail integration pushes drafts for you to send. Optional connector send (SMTP, Resend, SendGrid, or Postmark) and connector webhooks run only on Pro when you enable them in Settings → Agent defaults. You remain responsible for reviewing correspondence before it goes out.

## 5. Prohibited use

Do not use ClearTrace to bypass authentication, scrape behind logins, port-scan third parties, or conduct unauthorized security testing against sites you do not own.
